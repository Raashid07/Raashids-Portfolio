Problem & context

Why managing money feels overwhelming, and what Finmate set out to deliver.

Problem & Solution

Finmate — a finance management and learning app built to simplify money matters for everyday users, from research to UI execution.

Problem

Financially novice users often feel overwhelmed by expenses, confused about investments, and unaware of savings options. Existing finance apps are either too complex or lack guided learning, leaving users frustrated and disengaged.

Solution

Finmate provides a guided, user-friendly platform that combines:

Goal-based savings tools to build discipline · Smart expense tracking for better money control · Financial literacy modules to educate users step by step · Cross-platform accessibility across Android, iOS and Web.

With a design philosophy rooted in simplicity and clarity, Finmate empowers users to make confident financial decisions.

Project Overview

Finmate brings money tracking, goal-based saving and financial learning into one calm app — designed for people who find personal finance confusing, not for experts.

User Research & Insights

How I researched
01

Competitive analysis

Reviewed Vyapar and AngelOne to see how existing apps handle tracking, investing and learning.

02

Qualitative research

Spoke with everyday earners about how they save, invest and decide what to do with their money.

03

Affinity mapping

Grouped every observation into themes to find the patterns that repeat across users.

Key insights
01

Financial jargon blocks action

Terms like CAGR and ELSS make users hesitate — when they don't understand something, they don't invest.

Seen in AnjaliRajesh
Design opportunity

Plain-language explanations, right where decisions happen.

02

Too many choices overwhelm

Endless options and notifications push users to do nothing, or stick to what they already know.

Seen in AnjaliRajesh
Design opportunity

Curated, goal-based suggestions instead of long lists.

03

Trust comes from people

Decisions are made after asking friends, colleagues or finance influencers — not from inside the app.

Seen in AnjaliRajesh
Design opportunity

Relatable examples and transparent reasoning to build confidence.

04

Goals are vague and plans break

Users struggle to set specific savings goals, and irregular income derails the plans they do make.

Seen in RajeshPriya
Design opportunity

Flexible goals that adapt to changing income.

05

Learning must be bite-sized

Users want short, story-based lessons — in their own language — rather than dense financial content.

Seen in PriyaRajesh
Design opportunity

Short lessons and quizzes built into the app.

Testing & next iteration

Five people tried the prototype. Here is what testing found, where each issue came from, and what I’d change next.

10 issues found → what I’d change next 5 from test sessions, 1 from user research, 4 from a heuristic review. Only 09 is in the final screens; the rest are the next iteration and not yet designed.
01
Issue found

Financial terms like SIP and ELSS in the investment options after goal setup were confusing

Screen · Goal setup · Participant 1
Proposed change

Add tap-to-explain tooltips in plain language beside every financial term

02
Issue found

After adding an expense, there was no easy way to undo a mistake

Screen · Add Expense · Participant 2
Proposed change

Add an undo toast and swipe-to-edit / delete on transaction rows

03
Issue found

The dashboard showed too many numbers at once

Screen · Home · Participant 4
Proposed change

Lead with one key balance card; move detailed figures behind “View details”

04
Issue found

Expense categories took too long to pick from a long list

Screen · Add Expense · Heuristic review
Proposed change

Show recently used categories first, with icons and labels

05
Issue found

Goal setup assumed a fixed monthly income

Screen · Add Goals · Participant 5
Proposed change

Add flexible contributions so irregular earners can save what they can

06
Issue found

Goal progress wasn't obvious from the home screen

Screen · Goals · Heuristic review
Proposed change

Add progress rings and the remaining amount on each goal card

07
Issue found

Lessons felt long and were easy to abandon

Screen · Learning · Participant 3
Proposed change

Split lessons into short bite-sized chapters with a quick quiz at the end

08
Issue found

Users wanted content in their own language

Screen · Learning · User research
Proposed change

Add a language option for lessons and key app labels

09
Issue found

Income and expense tabs looked too similar

Screen · Money · Heuristic review
Proposed change

Gave income and expense distinct colours and icons for faster scanning (already in the final screens)

10
Issue found

Bottom navigation labels were unclear for some sections

Screen · Navigation · Heuristic review
Proposed change

Rename tabs to everyday words and keep icons with labels everywhere

Finmate aims to make money matters simple, understandable and stress-free for everyday users.

What I learned

  1. Plain language is a featureTerms like SIP and ELSS stopped testers mid-task. Explanations have to sit next to the decision, not in a help page.
  2. Lead with one numberThe dashboard showed too many numbers at once. I now design summary-first and keep detail one tap away.
  3. Make mistakes cheapTesters were wary of logging entries when undoing a wrong one was hard to find. An easy undo builds the confidence to act.
