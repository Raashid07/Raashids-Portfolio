Problem & context

Why regional art stays hard to find, and what ArtSphere set out to do.

Problem & Solution

Problem

Users often struggle to discover and explore diverse regional art due to scattered resources, limited access, and cultural barriers.

Solution

The ArtSphere app brings regional and cultural art forms into one accessible platform. It curates diverse art forms — visual, performing, cultural, and modern — through videos, stories, and interactive experiences.

Project Overview

The ArtSphere app helps people explore, experience and appreciate India’s regional and cultural art forms. It connects them to cultural heritage, traditional practices and the artists who keep them alive.

Usability testing

What a participant noticed while using the prototype.

Participant 1

Age24
EducationBachelor's Degree
ProfessionStudent
Task given

To explore art content in the app, using features like Video, Audio, Explore, and Workshop, and navigate to find content of interest efficiently.

Observations / Feedback

Workshop section was engaging but some descriptions were unclear.

Suggestions

Provide brief tooltips or descriptions for each workshop.

ArtSphere aims to empower local artists by connecting them with the right audience through a simple and intuitive digital experience.

What I learned

  1. Test earlier, with more peopleOne participant surfaced a real issue — unclear workshop descriptions — but one session can’t show a pattern. Next time I’d test a rough prototype with five people before polishing the UI.
  2. Copy is part of the interfaceThe workshop section itself worked; its descriptions were the friction. Writing short, clear descriptions belongs in the design work, not after it.
