UX Case StudyArtSphere

Discover Art from Around the Globe

Problem

Users often struggle to discover and explore diverse regional art due to scattered resources, limited access, and cultural barriers.

Solution

The ArtSphere App centralizes global art into one accessible digital platform. It curates diverse art forms — visual, performing, cultural, and modern — through videos, stories, and interactive experiences.

The ArtSphere App is designed to help users explore, experience, and appreciate diverse art forms from around the world. The app acts as a digital gateway to global creativity, connecting people to cultural heritage, traditional practices, and modern innovations in art.
