ArtSphere

A UI/UX case study focused on discovering and exploring regional and cultural art forms.

UI/UX Design User Research Mobile App Prototyping
Problem

Regional and cultural art forms are scattered and hard to find — most art apps favour mainstream galleries, so local traditions stay invisible.

Solution

A mobile app to browse art by region and tradition, with stories behind each form and the artists who keep it alive.

Design direction

Image-led and calm: the artwork carries the colour, while the interface stays quiet with clear, story-first navigation.

02 — Fintech app
Finmate
Fintech app · 2026

Finmate

A UI/UX case study focused on simplifying personal finance management.

UI/UX Design Fintech Mobile App User Experience
Problem

Managing money feels stressful — most finance apps bury people in charts, jargon and settings, so they stop checking in.

Solution

One clear home view of balance, spending and goals, with simple budgets and gentle nudges instead of dense reports.

User-focused approach

Built from user interviews: plain language, fewer steps per task and reassuring feedback so money feels in control.
